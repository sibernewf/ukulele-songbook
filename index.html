Notes on the Ukulele Fretboard

          
Standard tuning: G–C–E–A • Open strings through the 12th fret

        

        

        

          
Reading the chart: The 1st string is A, the string nearest the floor when playing. The 4th string is G, the string nearest your face.

          
Enharmonic notes: Notes such as C♯ and D♭ sound the same but have different names.
